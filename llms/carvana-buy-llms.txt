# Carvana Used Car Buying Guides

> Research guides for buying a used car: trim-level comparisons, generation
> timelines, and year-by-year changes for 629 models across 42
> makes - over 6,700 pages for model years 2009-2027, each linked to live Carvana inventory.

Pages follow a hub-and-spoke structure per model:

- Make directories: `/buy/{make}/` (e.g. `/buy/honda/`) - every covered model for a make
- Model hubs: `/buy/{make}-{model}/` (e.g. `/buy/honda-civic/`) - identity, strengths, generations, owner reviews
- Trim comparisons: `/buy/{make}-{model}/trims-compare/` - every trim level explained and compared
- Model years: `/buy/{year}-{make}-{model}/` (e.g. `/buy/2024-honda-civic/`) - what changed that year

Guides regenerate daily; this index was built 2026-08-13.
